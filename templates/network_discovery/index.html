Getting started

  1. Press Scan Now on a subnet below. {% if not can_write %}Scanning needs an admin. {% elif not scanner_ok %}The button appears once nmap is installed on the Jen host; the notice above says how.{% endif %}
  2. Discovery then checks every host it found against Kea's leases and reservations, the subnet's gateway, DNS, Kea and Jen addresses, IPAM Lite's entries, the devices Jen has seen and your known list, and calls what is left unknown.
  3. Its "Rogue Device" alert is sent for an unknown device the previous scan had not seen. Where it goes is set under {% if can_write %}Settings - Alerts{% else %}Settings - Alerts{% endif %}.
